**The Mesfin Tsige Lab** is a theoretical and computational polymer science group based in the Goodyear Polymer Center at the University of Akron, Akron, OH. Our team consists of researchers from diverse science and engineering backgrounds, united by a shared goal: to explore and understand some of the most fascinating phenomena in materials science, with a special focus on polymers. Our research spans a range of topics, including **soft condensed matter physics**, the **structure and dynamics of molecules** at surfaces and interfaces, the **elastic and failure behavior** of thermosetting polymers, **molecular transport** through nanostructured materials, and the **interaction of water with surfaces**.

Lab website: [blogs.uakron.edu/mtsige](https://blogs.uakron.edu/mtsige/)
